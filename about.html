About

Hi, I'm June Ren

I'm a Seattle-based food and restaurant photographer passionate about capturing the beauty, warmth, and craft behind every dish. Food is more than sustenance — it's culture, creativity, and connection, and I love helping restaurants and chefs tell that story through imagery.

My approach is warm, natural, and inviting. I want your guests to look at a photo and immediately feel hungry, curious, and ready to make a reservation. I work closely with chefs and restaurant teams to understand the vision behind each dish and translate that into images that convert.

Whether it's a cozy neighborhood cafe or an upscale dining experience, I bring the same care and attention to every shoot. Seattle's food scene is one of the most exciting in the country, and I'm proud to help showcase it.
